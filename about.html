About Us

Building a culture of ideas and execution.

Envision empowers the next generation of entrepreneurs by connecting students, mentors, investors, and industry leaders in one collaborative ecosystem.

Who We Are

We turn ambition into real opportunity.

Envision, the Entrepreneurship Cell of IIM Bodh Gaya, is devoted to cultivating an entrepreneurial mindset across campus and beyond. We connect students with the tools, expertise, and exposure needed to build future-ready businesses.

From ideation workshops and pitch events to mentor circles and incubation pathways, we help founders transform raw ideas into impactful ventures grounded in real-world problems.

Our Values

Entrepreneurship at the core of campus innovation.

Curiosity

We encourage questioning, exploration, and bold thinking that challenges the status quo.

Collaboration

We build networks across students, founders, corporations, and academia to unlock momentum.

Impact

We focus on ventures that create sustainable value for communities and industries.
