Competition Journey

Four rigorous rounds of evaluation.

Round 1

Executive Summary & Problem-Solution Fit (Online)

Teams submit a 3-page summary covering the target problem, unique value proposition, initial validation, and market potential.

Round 2

Pitch Deck & Financial Model Screening

Top 30 shortlisted teams submit a 10-slide deck with financial projections, unit economics, go-to-market plan, and competitive moat.

Round 3

Mentor Review & Deck Refinement Sprint

Top 15 finalist teams are paired with experienced industry mentors to stress-test their pitch and sharpen their presentation.

Grand Finale

Live Pitch & Jury Defense at IIM Bodh Gaya

Finalists pitch live (8 min presentation + 5 min Q&A) before a grand jury of venture capitalists, founders, and angel investors.

Evaluation Matrix

What the jury looks for in every pitch.

Innovation & Defensibility (25%)

Depth of insight into the problem, technological or operational novelty, and sustainability of the competitive advantage.

Market Opportunity (25%)

Total Addressable Market (TAM), serviceable market size, clear target persona, and realistic go-to-market strategy.

Business & Unit Economics (25%)

Monetization model, customer acquisition cost (CAC) vs. lifetime value (LTV), margin structure, and break-even path.

Team & Execution Capability (25%)

Complementary skill sets within the founding team, hustle demonstrated to date, prototype maturity, and coachability.
