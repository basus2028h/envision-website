About The Summit

Where ambitious ideas meet capital and guidance.

The Youth Entrepreneurship Summit (YES) is the marquee entrepreneurship conclave organized by Envision at IIM Bodh Gaya. YES serves as the epicenter for groundbreaking ideas, zero-to-one startup wisdom, and venture-building discussions.

Through keynote addresses by unicorn founders, interactive panel discussions on venture capital dynamics, investor demo days, and curated networking dinners, YES bridges the gap between passionate students and the broader startup ecosystem.

Summit Pillars

Curated tracks for founders & investors.

Founder Keynotes

Direct masterclasses and fireside conversations from pioneering founders sharing hard-earned zero-to-one execution blueprints.

Investor & VC Panels

Leading angel networks and venture capitalists break down the current funding environment, thesis evaluation, and growth metrics.

Live Startup Arena

Pre-screened collegiate and early-stage startup founders present their business models live to an attentive panel of investors.

1-on-1 Mentorship

Speed mentorship corners allowing aspiring student entrepreneurs to receive tailored feedback from seasoned domain leaders.

Schedule & Flow

A day packed with high-velocity learning.

Inaugural Address & Keynote

11:00 AM

Panel: Navigating Seed to Series A
