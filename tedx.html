The Platform

Conversations that challenge assumptions.

In the spirit of ideas worth spreading, TEDx IIM Bodh Gaya is an independently organized TED event operated under license from TED. We curate an eclectic mix of speakers who are rethinking industries, unlocking human potential, and advocating for systemic social impact.

From technological breakthroughs and climate solutions to philosophy, art, and resilience, our stage brings together voices that dare to imagine what lies beyond conventional boundaries.
