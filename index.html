Our Mission

Driving ideas that become ventures.

Envision is a non-profit student organization that aims at manifesting the entrepreneurial spirit of young students with a vision to promote innovation and good governance. Envision helps in the development of the entrepreneurial ecosystem by enabling interaction between its major components like students, entrepreneurs, mentors, angel investors, venture capital firms and the corporate world.

We also support them by providing necessary resources such as mentoring, consultancy and networking. We enhance the industry exposure of the students by inviting eminent personalities for guest talks and sharing ideas about the latest developments in their respective expertise.

We aim to set up mutually beneficial strategic partnerships with organizations looking for a long term relationship with a growing educational institution like ours. By providing hands on live projects we ensure that students live up to the expectation of ever evolving corporate world.

Vision

To metamorphose into a premier and productive resource centre for creation and diffusion of entrepreneurial learning and contribute to the development of a thriving entrepreneurial ecosystem that is inclusive of innovation, enterprise, sustainability and skill expansion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Learn more about E-Cell IIM Bodh Gaya, our incubation ecosystem, membership opportunities, and how we support startup founders.

What is E-Cell IIM Bodh Gaya?

E-Cell IIM Bodh Gaya is a student-run body dedicated to fostering an entrepreneurial mindset, driving startup culture, and providing the necessary incubation support, mentorship, and resources to aspiring student entrepreneurs within and outside the campus.

What is the core mission of E-Cell?

Our mission is to bridge the gap between academic learning and practical business execution by connecting innovators with industry leaders, investors, and mentors, ultimately turning visionary ideas into scalable ventures.

Who can join E-Cell?

Membership/recruitment is open to all enrolled students at IIM Bodh Gaya who are passionate about startups, venture creation, finance, marketing, or operations.

How can E-Cell help me if I have a startup idea?

We offer a robust ecosystem that includes early-stage incubation support, mentorship sessions with industry experts, networking opportunities with angel investors and VCs, and a platform to pitch your ideas during our flagship events.

Do I need a fully formed business plan to approach E-Cell?

Not at all! Whether you are at the napkin-sketch stage or have a functional Minimum Viable Product (MVP), we welcome ideas at all stages and help you refine and scale them.

How do I pitch my startup to investors through E-Cell?

Startups can register through our platform or participate in our annual flagship events and pitch fests. Shortlisted ventures get direct opportunities to pitch in front of angel networks, venture capitalists, and industry leaders.

What are the flagship events hosted by E-Cell IIM Bodh Gaya?

Our flagship events include our annual Entrepreneurship Summit (E-Summit), business plan competitions, Ideathons, panel discussions, and founder talk series featuring industry pioneers.

Are E-Cell events open to students from other B-schools and colleges?

Yes! Many of our major competitions, hackathons, and speaker sessions are open to national and international participants from diverse academic backgrounds.

How can I stay updated about upcoming E-Cell events and workshops?

You can subscribe to our newsletter, follow us on our official LinkedIn, Instagram, and Twitter handles, or check the "Events" section on this website.

How can industry professionals collaborate with E-Cell?

Industry experts can partner with us as mentors, guest speakers for our masterclasses, judges for pitch competitions, or sponsors for our annual flagship events.

How can investors get access to early-stage startups incubated or mentored by IIM Bodh Gaya?

Investors can partner with us for our curated deal-flow showcases, investor meets, and startup pitch sessions to discover high-potential ventures emerging from our ecosystem.
