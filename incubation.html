IIMBG INSPIRE

IIMBG Incubation Centre to Support Innovation, Research and Entrepreneurship

IIMBG Incubation Centre to Support Innovation, Research and Entrepreneurship (INSPIRE, in short) is a Section 8 company (Not for Profit) limited by guarantee under the Companies Act, 2013.

After beginning its operations in 2021, IIMBG INSPIRE is now one of the fastest growing incubators in India. We are based out of Bodh Gaya, Gaya – an aspirational district in the state of Bihar under the NITI Aayog programme.

IIMBG INSPIRE is the Incubation and Start up Hub of IIM Bodh Gaya. The aim is to build the Incubation Centre by leveraging the strengths of the region. Being a management-based incubator, IIMBG INSPIRE is sector agnostic and can support ventures in all sectors. However, in the direction of becoming a world class incubation centre, IIMBG INSPIRE wants to strategically scale up its existing programs and add new programs to its portfolio of services.

IIMBG INSPIRE is a platform for participants and incubatees to access the diverse resources of the Institute and the entrepreneurial community associated with IIM BG. INSPIRE strives to be the primary focal point for students, working professionals, startup enthusiasts, budding entrepreneurs and the larger entrepreneurial community interested in innovation and finding a home for launching immersive experiences that are scalable businesses.

The ever-growing inclination among the youth to become job creator and to do something of their own, start ups have become the path to do the same and give something back to the society. But these fledglings start up universe is chaotic and unorganized, so these fledgling start up needs clear vision along with superior value proposition and business model that is rational and pragmatic as per the current market scenario.

IIMBG INSPIRE aims to provide focused, faster and speed growth to new ventures for their successful commercialization of product/technology through a combination of Seed Capital, Corporate Venture Capital Fund, Smart Working Space, Corporate Market Access, Mentoring and Training programs. Our Programs are designed to help these fledgling start ups succeed and bring meaningful change in the society.

Vision & Mission

Anchored in purpose, built for sustainable scale.

Our Vision

To metamorphose into a premier resource centre for the creation and diffusion of entrepreneurial learning that contribute to the development of a thriving entrepreneurial ecosystem.

Our Mission

IIMBG INSPIRE aims to provide support to the entrepreneurs through their early stages of business development by building an ecosystem that is inclusive of innovation, enterprise sustainability and social value.

Strategic Roadmap

Our Goals and Objectives

Driving incubation, regional momentum, and cross-sector innovation across India.

Agri & Rural Incubation

Promote and establish agri and rural incubation centre in Bihar through parallel portfolio programs.

Core Sector Innovation

Support and encourage both innovative technology-based agri startups as well as modern traditional businesses that have deep economic impact.

Full-Stack Founder Support

Provide start-ups with necessary guidance, tech support, infrastructure, access to investors, networking, and scaling resources.

Regional Ecosystem Catalyst

Foster a vibrant, resilient entrepreneurial ecosystem in Bihar and its vicinity.

Multi-Sector Breadth

Cover Agriculture, FMCG, Fashion & Apparels, IT/IoT & AI, E-Commerce, Tourism & Hospitality, Automobile, Healthcare, and Waste Management.

Youth & Women Entrepreneurship

Inspire youths to pursue venture creation, encouraging Women entrepreneurship and campus student startups.

Strategic Growth Network

Build a collaborative network of institutions, corporates, investors, researchers, and mentors to accelerate growth.

Commercialization Pipeline

Identify high-potential entrepreneurial opportunities and guide them through structured commercialization.

District-Level Entrepreneurship Development Programs (EDPs)

Build competencies in Startup Ecosystem Enablers (Entrepreneurs, Faculty, Students of K-12 and Engineering/Science Colleges, and E-Cell/E-Lab staff), aiming to establish Entrepreneurial & Skill Development centres at the district level nationwide.
